Why this book

Getting into medical school can feel like a maze of unwritten rules. Premed Mentor sets them out plainly, so you can spend less energy guessing and more energy building a life and an application you're proud of.

01

Plan with clarity

Know what matters in each year, from first-year courses to application season.

02

Build real experience

Find research, volunteering and clinical work that you care about.

03

Apply with confidence

Approach the MCAT, CASPer, essays and interviews with a clear strategy.
