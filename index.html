Why a Canadian guide matters

Much of the premed advice out there is American, or comes from people who left Canada or never applied here. But getting into a Canadian medical school works differently: provincial residency rules, school-by-school application systems, Casper, MMIs, French-language programs and a famously competitive applicant pool. Premed Mentor has a singular focus: the Canadian premed.
